# Exercise 5.6: Least-squares certificates for Chebyshev approximation

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.6](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=288). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

## (a) Norm-comparison bound

Least-squares optimality and the supplied norm inequalities give

```math
\|Ax_{\mathrm{ls}}-b\|_\infty
\le\|Ax_{\mathrm{ls}}-b\|_2
\le\|Ax_{\mathrm{ch}}-b\|_2
\le\sqrt m\,\|Ax_{\mathrm{ch}}-b\|_\infty.
```

Thus the known value divided by $`\sqrt m`$ is a lower bound on the Chebyshev optimum.

## (b) Two dual residuals

Let $`r=b-Ax_{\mathrm{ls}}\ne0`$. The least-squares normal equations give $`A^Tr=0`$. Both $`\widehat\nu=-r/\|r\|_1`$ and $`\widetilde\nu=r/\|r\|_1`$ therefore satisfy $`A^T\nu=0`$ and $`\|\nu\|_1=1`$, so both are dual feasible. Since $`b=r+Ax_{\mathrm{ls}}`$,

```math
b^Tr=\|r\|_2^2,
\qquad
b^T\widetilde\nu=\frac{\|r\|_2^2}{\|r\|_1}>0,
\qquad
b^T\widehat\nu=-\frac{\|r\|_2^2}{\|r\|_1}<0.
```

The positive-sign residual gives the better bound; the negative one is worse even than the trivial zero lower bound. Moreover

```math
\frac{\|r\|_2^2}{\|r\|_1}
\ge\frac{\|r\|_2}{\sqrt m}
\ge\frac{\|r\|_\infty}{\sqrt m},
```

so its dual certificate is at least as strong as the lower bound in (a). It is also at most $`\|r\|_\infty`$, because $`\sum_i r_i^2\le\|r\|_\infty\sum_i|r_i|`$. Hence the computable suboptimality certificate is

```math
0\le\|Ax_{\mathrm{ls}}-b\|_\infty-p^\star
\le\|r\|_\infty-\frac{\|r\|_2^2}{\|r\|_1}.
```

If $`r=0`$, both primal norms are zero and least squares is already Chebyshev-optimal; take the zero dual vector instead of dividing by zero.

## Validation

The residual convention is $`b-Ax`$, matching the positive $`b^T\nu`$ dual objective. The comparison proves dominance of the new lower bound, not merely feasibility.
