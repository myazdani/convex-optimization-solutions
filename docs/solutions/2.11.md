# Exercise 2.11: Convexity of product superlevel sets

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 2.11, p. 61](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=75).

Original solution.

For $`n\ge1`$, every member of the set has strictly positive coordinates, because its nonnegative coordinate product is at least one. Take feasible $`x,y`$ and $`0<t<1`$. Weighted arithmetic–geometric mean gives, coordinatewise,
```math
tx_i+(1-t)y_i\ge x_i^t y_i^{1-t}.
```
Multiplying these nonnegative inequalities yields
```math
\prod_{i=1}^n(tx_i+(1-t)y_i)
\ge\left(\prod_i x_i\right)^t\left(\prod_i y_i\right)^{1-t}\ge1.
```
The convex combination remains in the nonnegative orthant and is feasible. The endpoint weights are immediate. This proves the general claim and, with $`n=2`$, the original hyperbolic-set claim.
