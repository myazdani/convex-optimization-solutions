# Exercise 2.32: Dual of a finitely generated cone

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 2.32, p. 64](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=78).

Original solution.

For $`K=\{Ax:x\ge0\}\subseteq\mathbb R^m`$,
```math
K^*=\{y\in\mathbb R^m:A^Ty\ge0\}.
```
Indeed, $`y\in K^*`$ means $`y^TAx=(A^Ty)^Tx\ge0`$ for every nonnegative $`x`$. Necessity follows by testing each coordinate vector $`x=e_i`$. Sufficiency follows because the inner product of two componentwise nonnegative vectors is nonnegative. No full-rank or nonzero-column assumption on $`A`$ is needed.
