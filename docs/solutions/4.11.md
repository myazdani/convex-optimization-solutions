# Exercise 4.11: Linear programs for one and infinity norms

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 4.11](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=207). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

In the following LPs all variables are unrestricted except for the explicitly given inequalities. The epigraph inequalities automatically force the relevant bound variables to be nonnegative.

## (a) Infinity-norm residual

```math
\mathrm{minimize}\quad t\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad -t\mathbf1\preceq Ax-b\preceq t\mathbf1.
```

For fixed $`x`$, the least feasible $`t`$ is $`\|Ax-b\|_\infty`$.

## (b) One-norm residual

```math
\mathrm{minimize}\quad\mathbf1^Tu\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad -u\preceq Ax-b\preceq u.
```

For fixed $`x`$, the least feasible components are $`u_i=|(Ax-b)_i|`$, giving exactly the one norm.

## (c) One-norm residual in a unit box

```math
\mathrm{minimize}\quad\mathbf1^Tu\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad -u\preceq Ax-b\preceq u,\qquad
-\mathbf1\preceq x\preceq\mathbf1.
```

The additional inequalities are exactly the stated infinity-norm bound on $`x`$.

## (d) One-norm variable with bounded residual

```math
\mathrm{minimize}\quad\mathbf1^Tu\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad -u\preceq x\preceq u,\qquad
-\mathbf1\preceq Ax-b\preceq\mathbf1.
```

The last inequalities are exactly the residual constraint; for fixed feasible $`x`$ the minimum over $`u`$ is its one norm.

## (e) Sum of two norms

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{minimize}\quad &\mathbf1^Tu+t\\
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad &-u\preceq Ax-b\preceq u,\\
&-t\mathbf1\preceq x\preceq t\mathbf1.
\end{aligned}
```

For fixed $`x`$, choose $`u=|Ax-b|`$ and $`t=\|x\|_\infty`$ to attain the original objective.

## Complete solution correspondence

In every case, discard the auxiliary variables of an LP optimizer to recover an optimizer of the original problem. Indeed, every original-feasible point has the exact lift given above, and every LP-feasible point has objective at least its original objective. These two inequalities prove equality of values and optimality of both maps. At an LP optimum every auxiliary bound appearing with positive objective coefficient must be tight to its corresponding absolute value or norm; otherwise reducing it strictly improves the objective.

Problems (a) and (b) attain their minima because the residuals range over a closed affine subspace and a norm has compact bounded sublevel sets in residual space. Problem (c) is continuous on a nonempty compact box. Problem (d), when feasible, attains by the compact sublevel sets of $`\|x\|_1`$; otherwise both formulations are infeasible. Problem (e) is continuous and coercive because of $`\|x\|_\infty`$. The arguments assume the usual positive row and column dimensions.

## Validation

All five epigraph constructions are affine, and both directions of the optimizer correspondence and attainment conditions are established.
