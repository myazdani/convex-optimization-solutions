# Exercise 2.24: Explicit supporting halfspaces

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 2.24, p. 63](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=77).

Original solution.

## (a) The positive hyperbola region

An exact representation is
```math
C=\bigcap_{t>0}\{x:t x_1+x_2/t\ge2\}.
```
Every point of $`C`$ satisfies each inequality by arithmetic–geometric mean. Conversely, those inequalities force $`x_1\ge0`$ (let $`t\to\infty`$) and $`x_2\ge0`$ (let $`t\downarrow0`$). Neither coordinate can be zero: with one zero, taking the corresponding limit makes the left side tend to zero. For positive coordinates, minimizing the left side over $`t>0`$ gives $`2\sqrt{x_1x_2}`$, so all inequalities together imply $`x_1x_2\ge1`$. Each boundary line touches at $`(1/t,t)`$, and is its tangent line.

## (b) Supporting hyperplanes of the cube

Orient a support as $`a^T(x-\widehat x)\le0`$ on the cube, with $`a\ne0`$. Since $`\sup_{\|x\|_\infty\le1}a^Tx=\sum_i|a_i|`$, the support condition is
```math
\sum_i a_i\widehat x_i=\sum_i|a_i|.
```
Each term on the left is at most its corresponding term on the right. Equality therefore holds exactly when
```math
a_i=0\quad\text{if }|\widehat x_i|\lt 1,\qquad
 a_i\ge0\quad\text{if }\widehat x_i=1,\qquad
 a_i\le0\quad\text{if }\widehat x_i=-1.
```
Every nonzero vector satisfying these conditions gives the hyperplane $`a^Tx=a^T\widehat x`$, and these are all the supports. Equivalently the outward normals are nonzero nonnegative combinations of the active facet normals $`\widehat x_i e_i`$.
