# Exercise 10.5: A quadratic equality penalty leaves Newton directions unchanged

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 10.5, printed page 558. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=572).

At a feasible point $`Ax=b`$, adding $`(Ax-b)^TQ(Ax-b)`$ leaves the objective gradient unchanged and changes the Hessian from $`H`$ to $`H+2A^TQA`$. Every feasible Newton direction satisfies $`Av=0`$, so both its quadratic-model value and the KKT equations are unchanged:
```math
(H+2A^TQA)v+A^Tw=Hv+A^Tw=-\nabla f(x),\qquad Av=0.
```
Thus the unique Newton direction and the quadratic-model multiplier are exactly the same, whenever the original KKT system is nonsingular. The added PSD term also cannot alter nonsingularity on the feasible nullspace.

In fact the primal-dual infeasible-start Newton equations give the same increments too when evaluated at the same $`(x,\nu)`$: writing $`r_p=Ax-b`$, the gradient gains $`2A^TQr_p`$ and the Hessian gains $`2A^TQA`$, but the equation $`Av=-r_p`$ makes these additions cancel. This algebraic invariance of the direction does not assert invariance of an infeasible-start line search based on the changed residual norm.
