# Exercise 5.27: Equality-constrained least squares in closed form

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.27](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=296). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Let $`H=A^TA\succ0`$, $`x_{\mathrm{ls}}=H^{-1}A^Tb`$, and $`M=GH^{-1}G^T`$. Since $`G`$ has full row rank, $`M\succ0`$: for nonzero $`v`$, $`G^Tv\ne0`$ and $`v^TMv=(G^Tv)^TH^{-1}(G^Tv)>0`$.

For the Lagrangian $`L(x,\nu)=\|Ax-b\|_2^2+\nu^T(Gx-h)`$, the KKT equations are

```math
2H(x-x_{\mathrm{ls}})+G^T\nu=0,\qquad Gx=h.
```

There are no multiplier sign or complementarity conditions for equality constraints. Solving the first equation and substituting into the second gives

```math
\nu^\star=2M^{-1}(Gx_{\mathrm{ls}}-h),
\qquad
x^\star=x_{\mathrm{ls}}+H^{-1}G^TM^{-1}(h-Gx_{\mathrm{ls}}).
```

These formulas are defined because both inverses above exist. They satisfy both equations directly. Convexity makes KKT sufficient, and full-row-rank $`G`$ ensures affine feasibility for every $`h`$, so affine-equality regularity makes them necessary as well. Strong convexity in $`x`$ and positive definiteness of $`M`$ give unique primal and dual solutions.

For an additional value check, complete the least-squares square:

```math
p^\star=\|Ax_{\mathrm{ls}}-b\|_2^2
+(h-Gx_{\mathrm{ls}})^TM^{-1}(h-Gx_{\mathrm{ls}}).
```

The second term is the exact objective increase needed to satisfy the equalities. The dual function, obtained by minimizing the Lagrangian quadratic, is

```math
g(\nu)=b^Tb-h^T\nu-
(A^Tb-\tfrac12G^T\nu)^TH^{-1}(A^Tb-\tfrac12G^T\nu),
```

and substitution of $`\nu^\star`$ gives the same value.

## Validation

All factors of two correspond to the unsquared-coefficient objective $`\|Ax-b\|^2`$, and the multiplier sign follows from $`Gx-h`$.
