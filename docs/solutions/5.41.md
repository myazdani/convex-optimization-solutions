# Exercise 5.41: Dual of the fastest mixing Markov chain problem

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.41](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=300). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Write $`J=\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n`$, and let $`C_E`$ be the cone of symmetric entrywise nonnegative matrices supported on the undirected edge set $`E`$. Eliminating $`t`$ gives

```math
\mathrm{minimize}\quad \|P-J\|_2
\qquad\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad P\in C_E,\quad P\mathbf1=\mathbf1.
```

The spectral norm is appropriate because $`P-J`$ is symmetric. Introduce a symmetric variable $`Q=P-J`$, a symmetric equality multiplier $`Y`$ for $`P-J-Q=0`$, and $`z\in\mathbf R^n`$ for $`\mathbf1-P\mathbf1=0`$. Keeping $`P\in C_E`$ as the domain, the Lagrangian is

```math
\begin{aligned}
L(P,Q,Y,z)
&=\|Q\|_2-\mathrm{tr}(YQ)
+\mathrm{tr}\left(\left[Y-\frac{z\mathbf1^T+\mathbf1z^T}{2}\right]P\right)
+\mathbf1^Tz-\mathrm{tr}(YJ).
\end{aligned}
```

Here $`z^TP\mathbf1=\mathrm{tr}((z\mathbf1^T+\mathbf1z^T)P/2)`$ uses symmetry of $`P`$. The infimum over $`Q`$ is zero exactly when $`\|Y\|_{2*}\le1`$, and is $`-\infty`$ otherwise: the dual-norm inequality proves the first assertion, and scaling a direction with $`\mathrm{tr}(YQ)>\|Q\|_2`$ proves the second. For symmetric $`Y`$, diagonalizing it shows $`\|Y\|_{2*}=\sum_i|\lambda_i(Y)|`$.

The infimum over $`P\in C_E`$ is zero exactly when

```math
Y_{ij}\ge\frac{z_i+z_j}{2}\qquad ((i,j)\in E).
```

Indeed, each allowed nonnegative diagonal entry has coefficient $`Y_{ii}-z_i`$, and each allowed off-diagonal pair has coefficient $`2Y_{ij}-z_i-z_j`$. A negative coefficient can be scaled to give $`-\infty`$. Thus the dual is

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{maximize}\quad &\mathbf1^Tz-\frac1n\mathbf1^TY\mathbf1\\
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad &\|Y\|_{2*}\le1,\\
&\frac{z_i+z_j}{2}\le Y_{ij}\quad ((i,j)\in E).
\end{aligned}
```

Equivalently, substitute $`z=2\widetilde z`$ to obtain edge constraints $`\widetilde z_i+\widetilde z_j\le Y_{ij}`$ and objective $`2\mathbf1^T\widetilde z-\mathbf1^TY\mathbf1/n`$.

## Normalization error in the printed target

The source combines the latter edge constraint with the former objective, omitting a factor of two. They cannot be combined this way. For $`n=1`$ and the single self-edge, the primal forces $`P=1`$ and has value zero. The printed dual permits $`Y=-1,z=-1/2`$, with objective $`z-Y=1/2`$, contradicting weak duality. The corrected forms above remove this inconsistency.

When the stochastic support constraints are feasible, the feasible set of $`P`$ is compact and the primal optimum is attained. Strong duality also holds: it is minimization of a continuous finite norm over a nonempty polyhedron, so the convex normal-cone optimality condition and the polyhedral multiplier representation give an attaining dual certificate. In particular, one need not assume all allowed edges admit strictly positive weights. None of these regularity observations can repair the printed factor error.

## Validation

The trace expansion checks diagonal and off-diagonal coefficients separately. A one-state chain gives an exact counterexample to the printed normalization and an exact check of the corrected dual.
