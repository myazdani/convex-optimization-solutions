# Exercise 11.17: Log homogeneity of the dual logarithm

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 11.17, printed page 628. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=642).

Use a bar to distinguish the dual logarithm:
```math
\bar\psi(v)=\inf_{u\in\mathrm{int}K}\{v^Tu-\psi(u)\}.
```
For $`s>0`$, substitute $`z=su`$. Positive scaling is a bijection of the cone interior, and $`\psi(z/s)=\psi(z)-\theta\log s`$. Therefore
```math
\begin{aligned}
\bar\psi(sv)
&=\inf_{u\in\mathrm{int}K}\{sv^Tu-\psi(u)\}\\
&=\inf_{z\in\mathrm{int}K}\{v^Tz-\psi(z)+\theta\log s\}\\
&=\bar\psi(v)+\theta\log s.
\end{aligned}
```
For $`v\in\mathrm{int}K^*`$ these values are finite by the dual-logarithm definition. The calculation does not require choosing or differentiating an optimizer and proves that the degree remains exactly $`\theta`$.
