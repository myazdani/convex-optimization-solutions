# Exercise 3.50: Log-concavity of polynomial coefficients in negative roots

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.50, p. 123](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=137).

Original solution.

Put $`x_j=-\lambda_j>0`$. Expanding $`\prod_j(s+x_j)`$ shows that the coefficient $`a_k(\lambda)`$ is exactly the elementary symmetric polynomial $`S_k(x)`$, for $`k=1,\ldots,n`$.

By the theorem supplied in the hint, $`H_k(x)=S_k(x)^{1/k}`$ is concave and positive on the positive orthant. The logarithm is concave and increasing, so $`\log H_k`$ is concave. Multiplying by $`k>0`$ gives
```math
\log S_k(x)=k\log H_k(x),
```
which is concave. Finally $`x=-\lambda`$ is affine, so $`\lambda\mapsto\log a_k(\lambda)`$ is concave on the negative orthant. Hence every stated coefficient is log-concave in the negative roots. The leading coefficient is the constant one and is log-concave as well. The conclusion uses the supplied elementary-symmetric-function theorem; it does not follow merely from positivity of the coefficients of $`S_k`$.
