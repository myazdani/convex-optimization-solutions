# Exercise 6.4: A posteriori error bound for smooth one-norm fitting

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 6.4, printed page 345. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=359).

Write $`F(x)=\sum_i\sqrt{r_i^2+\epsilon}`$, with $`r=Ax-b`$. Its scalar summands have second derivative $`\epsilon/(r_i^2+\epsilon)^{3/2}>0`$. Full column rank makes $`F`$ strictly convex and coercive, so its minimizer exists and is unique. Define
```math
q_i=\frac{\hat r_i}{\sqrt{\hat r_i^2+\epsilon}}.
```
Stationarity gives $`A^Tq=0`$; also $`|q_i|<1`$.

## (a) Lower bound
For every $`x`$, componentwise $`|r_i|\ge q_i r_i`$. Therefore
```math
\|Ax-b\|_1\ge q^T(Ax-b)=-q^Tb
=q^T\hat r=\sum_i\frac{\hat r_i^2}{\sqrt{\hat r_i^2+\epsilon}}.
```
Taking the infimum establishes the requested lower bound, without assuming any sign pattern for the residuals.

## (b) Suboptimality certificate
Subtract the lower bound from $`\sum_i|\hat r_i|`$:
```math
\|A\hat x-b\|_1-p^\star
\le\sum_i\left(|\hat r_i|-\frac{\hat r_i^2}{\sqrt{\hat r_i^2+\epsilon}}\right)
=\sum_i|\hat r_i|\left(1-\frac{|\hat r_i|}{\sqrt{\hat r_i^2+\epsilon}}\right).
```
This includes zero residuals without a limiting convention. The certificate uses the actual fitted residual, rather than a worst-case replacement such as $`m\sqrt\epsilon`$.
