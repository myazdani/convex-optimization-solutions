# Exercise 2.15: Convex restrictions on a discrete distribution

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 2.15, p. 62](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=76).

Original solution.

All feasible sets below also include the simplex constraints $`p\ge0`$ and $`\mathbf1^Tp=1`$.

## (a) Bounds on an expectation

Convex. With $`f_i=f(a_i)`$, the constraints are $`\alpha\le f^Tp\le\beta`$, two affine inequalities.

## (b) Tail probability

Convex. The constraint is $`\sum_{i:a_i>\alpha}p_i\le\beta`$.

## (c) Comparison of absolute moments

Convex. Rewrite it as $`\sum_i(|a_i|^3-\alpha|a_i|)p_i\le0`$.

## (d) Upper bound on the second moment

Convex: $`\sum_i a_i^2p_i\le\alpha`$ is linear.

## (e) Lower bound on the second moment

Also convex: $`-\sum_i a_i^2p_i\le-\alpha`$ is linear.

## (f) Upper bound on variance

Not convex in general. With two distinct support values and $`\alpha=0`$, either point mass has variance zero, while their equal mixture has variance $`(a_2-a_1)^2/4>0`$.

## (g) Lower bound on variance

Convex. If $`\mu_p=\sum_i a_ip_i`$ and $`v(p)=\sum_i a_i^2p_i-\mu_p^2`$, direct expansion gives
```math
v(tp+(1-t)q)=tv(p)+(1-t)v(q)+t(1-t)(\mu_p-\mu_q)^2.
```
Thus variance is concave in the distribution, and its superlevel sets are convex.

## (h) Lower bound on the first quartile

Convex, although generally not closed. The first quartile is the earliest support value whose cumulative probability reaches $`1/4`$. It is at least $`\alpha`$ exactly when
```math
\sum_{i:a_i<\alpha}p_i<\frac14.
```
To check the strict inequality, if this sum reaches $`1/4`$, some support value strictly below $`\alpha`$ already attains the threshold. If it is less than $`1/4`$, none can. A strict affine halfspace is convex.

## (i) Upper bound on the first quartile

Convex. It is equivalent to
```math
\sum_{i:a_i\le\alpha}p_i\ge\frac14,
```
a closed affine halfspace. These characterizations also cover thresholds outside the support, where the feasible set becomes empty or the entire simplex.
