# Exercise 3.27: Concavity of Cholesky diagonal entries

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.27, p. 118](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=132).

Original solution.

For $`i=1`$, the entry is $`L_{11}=\sqrt{X_{11}}`$, a concave function of the positive scalar $`X_{11}`$.

For $`i>1`$, use the leading principal block $`\begin{bmatrix}Y&z\\z^T&w\end{bmatrix}\succ0`$. The function $`z^TY^{-1}z`$ is convex jointly in $`z,Y\succ0`$. For example its variational expression
```math
z^TY^{-1}z=\sup_u\{2u^Tz-u^TYu\}
```
is a supremum of affine functions of $`(z,Y)`$, with equality attained at $`u=Y^{-1}z`$. Consequently $`w-z^TY^{-1}z`$ is concave, and it is positive by the Schur complement of the leading positive definite block. The square root is concave and increasing on positive numbers, so
```math
L_{ii}=\sqrt{w-z^TY^{-1}z}
```
is concave by composition. Taking a leading submatrix is affine in $`X`$, which proves the desired concavity on the full positive definite cone.
