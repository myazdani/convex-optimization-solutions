# Exercise 4.5: Three formulations of the Huber loss

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 4.5](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=204). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Let $`r_i=a_i^Tx-b_i`$. It suffices to minimize each lifted scalar penalty while holding $`x`$ fixed.

## (a)–(b): Optimal weights

For a scalar residual $`r`$, let $`q(w)=r^2/(1+w)+M^2w`$, $`w\ge0`$. Its derivative is $`M^2-r^2/(1+w)^2`$. This derivative is nondecreasing, and the minimum is attained at

```math
w^\star(r)=\max\{|r|/M-1,0\}.
```

Substitution gives $`r^2`$ when $`|r|\le M`$ and $`2M|r|-M^2`$ otherwise, exactly the Huber penalty. Thus the partial minimum in (b) equals the objective in (a), for every $`x`$. Its denominator domain is automatic when $`w\ge0`$. The quadratic-over-linear terms are convex jointly in the residual and $`1+w>0`$, so (b) is convex.

## (a)–(c): Optimal split

The two-sided constraint for a scalar residual is $`|r|\le u+v`$. For fixed $`u\in[0,M]`$, the best $`v`$ is $`\max(|r|-u,0)`$ because its coefficient is positive. The remaining objective is

```math
u^2+2M\max(|r|-u,0).
```

If $`|r|\le M`$, its derivative on $`[0,|r|]`$ is $`2u-2M\le0`$ and it is increasing on $`[|r|,M]`$. If $`|r|>M`$, the derivative is $`2u-2M\le0`$ throughout the interval. Hence the minimum is attained at

```math
u^\star(r)=\min(|r|,M),\qquad v^\star(r)=\max(|r|-M,0),
```

with value equal to the Huber penalty in both cases. The quadratic objective has a positive semidefinite Hessian and all constraints are affine, so (c) is a convex QP.

## Solution maps, values, and existence

From an optimizer $`x^\star`$ of (a), the displayed coordinatewise formulas produce optimizers of (b) and (c). From an optimizer of either lifted problem, retain its $`x`$: partial minimization shows its Huber objective is at most the lifted optimum, while lifting every original point gives the reverse bound. Therefore it solves (a), and all three values coincide. The lifted variables must in fact minimize their scalar terms, or replacing them would improve the objective.

These problems attain their minima: the Huber objective is continuous and coercive in the residual vector, and the residuals range over the closed affine set $`\mathrm{range}(A)-b`$. A minimizing residual exists there, and has a preimage $`x`$. Null directions of $`A`$ do not affect this argument.

## Validation

The maps include residuals equal to zero or exactly at the threshold. Each identity was derived by scalar minimization rather than a numerical comparison.
