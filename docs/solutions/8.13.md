# Exercise 8.13: The covering ellipsoid of a simplex

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 8.13, printed page 449. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=463).

For the vertices $`v_0=0,v_i=e_i`$, set
```math
c=\frac1{n+1}\mathbf1,\qquad
Q=\frac n{n+1}\left(I-\frac1{n+1}\mathbf1\mathbf1^T\right)\succ0.
```
The proposed ellipsoid is $`E=\{x:(x-c)^TQ^{-1}(x-c)\le1\}`$. Since
```math
Q^{-1}=\frac{n+1}{n}(I+\mathbf1\mathbf1^T),
```
substitution shows that every vertex lies on its boundary. Convexity then makes $`E`$ cover the simplex.

To prove minimum volume, consider any covering ellipsoid $`\|Ax+b\|\le1`$ with $`A\succ0`$. The vertices have mean $`c`$ and covariance $`Q/n`$. Averaging their squared containment constraints gives
```math
\frac1n\mathrm{tr}(AQA)+\|Ac+b\|^2\le1.
```
The arithmetic–geometric mean inequality for the positive eigenvalues gives
```math
\det(AQA)^{1/n}\le\frac1n\mathrm{tr}(AQA)\le1,
\qquad \det A\le(\det Q)^{-1/2}.
```
This bounds its volume below by the volume of $`E`$, which attains equality with $`A=Q^{-1/2},b=-Ac`$. It is the Löwner–John ellipsoid.

Shrink about $`c`$ by a factor $`\rho`$. Containment in $`x_i\ge0`$ requires
```math
c_i-\rho\sqrt{Q_{ii}}\ge0.
```
Here $`c_i=1/(n+1)`$ and $`\sqrt{Q_{ii}}=n/(n+1)`$, so every such facet requires $`\rho\le1/n`$. For the last facet $`\mathbf1^Tx\le1`$, the support condition is
```math
\mathbf1^Tc+\rho\sqrt{\mathbf1^TQ\mathbf1}\le1,
```
with $`\mathbf1^Tc=n/(n+1)`$ and the same square root $`n/(n+1)`$. It gives exactly the same restriction. Thus $`\rho=1/n`$ fits, and every larger factor fails at a facet. For $`n=1`$ no shrinking is necessary, as expected.
