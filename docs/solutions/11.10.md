# Exercise 11.10: The objective-bound parametrization of the central path

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 11.10, printed page 626. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=640).

Suppose the centers in the question exist and are unique on their strict domains. At the augmented analytic center $`z=z_*(u)`$, its stationarity equations are
```math
\frac1{u-f_0(z)}\nabla f_0(z)+\nabla\phi(z)+A^T\nu=0,
\qquad Az=b.
```
The scalar $`t=1/(u-f_0(z))`$ is positive. These are precisely the central-path equations at $`t`$, and convexity makes them sufficient, hence $`z_*(u)=x_*(t)`$.

Conversely take a central point $`x=x_*(t)`$ and set
```math
u_{\rm level}=f_0(x)+1/t.
```
This level is larger than both $`f_0(x)`$ and $`p^\star`$. The point lies in its augmented strict domain, and its central stationarity equations become the augmented-center equations because $`1/(u_{\rm level}-f_0(x))=t`$. Convexity and uniqueness give $`z_*(u_{\rm level})=x_*(t)`$. The notation $`u_{\rm level}`$ avoids confusing this scalar objective bound with the equality multiplier $`\nu`$. These two constructions prove equality of the curves, including all points rather than only a limiting correspondence.
