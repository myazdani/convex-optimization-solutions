# Exercise 2.33: The monotone nonnegative cone and its dual

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 2.33, p. 64](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=78).

Original solution.

## (a) Properness

The cone is the intersection of the homogeneous halfspaces $`x_i-x_{i+1}\ge0`$ for $`i<n`$ and $`x_n\ge0`$, so it is closed and convex. It has interior: any vector with strictly decreasing positive coordinates, for example $`(n,n-1,\ldots,1)`$, satisfies all inequalities strictly. If both $`x`$ and $`-x`$ belong, componentwise nonnegativity forces $`x=0`$. Hence it is pointed and proper.

## (b) Dual

Set $`x_{n+1}=0`$, $`d_j=x_j-x_{j+1}\ge0`$, and $`s_j=\sum_{i=1}^j y_i`$. Summation by parts gives
```math
x^Ty=\sum_{j=1}^n d_j s_j.
```
Every nonnegative vector $`d`$ occurs from exactly one cone member, namely $`x_i=\sum_{j=i}^n d_j`$. The expression is therefore nonnegative for every cone member exactly when every $`s_j\ge0`$. Thus
```math
K_{m+}^*=\left\{y:\sum_{i=1}^j y_i\ge0,
\quad j=1,\ldots,n\right\}.
```
Necessity can also be checked on the vectors whose first $`j`$ entries are one and remaining entries are zero.
