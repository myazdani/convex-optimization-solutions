# Exercise 3.49: Four log-concave functions

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.49, p. 122](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=136).

Original solution.

## (a) Logistic function

Its logarithm is $`\log f(x)=x-\log(1+e^x)`$, an affine function minus a convex log-sum-exp. Equivalently its second derivative is $`-e^x/(1+e^x)^2<0`$. Hence it is log-concave.

## (b) Harmonic mean

Exercise 3.17 with $`p=-1`$ shows $`f(x)=(\sum_i1/x_i)^{-1}`$ is concave and positive on the positive orthant. Composition with the increasing concave logarithm makes $`\log f`$ concave, proving log-concavity.

## (c) Product divided by sum

Put $`S=\sum_i x_i>0`$. Then $`\log f=\sum_i\log x_i-\log S`$, whose Hessian quadratic form in a direction $`v`$ is
```math
-\sum_i\frac{v_i^2}{x_i^2}+\frac{(\sum_i v_i)^2}{S^2}.
```
Cauchy–Schwarz gives $`(\sum_i v_i)^2\le(\sum_i v_i^2/x_i^2)(\sum_i x_i^2)`$, and $`\sum_i x_i^2\le S^2`$. Therefore the form is nonpositive. This proves concavity of the log, including $`n=1`$, where the original function is constant one.

## (d) Determinant divided by trace

Its logarithm is $`h(X)=\log\det X-\log\mathrm{tr}X`$. For a symmetric direction $`V`$,
```math
D^2h(X)[V,V]
=-\mathrm{tr}(X^{-1}VX^{-1}V)
+\frac{(\mathrm{tr}V)^2}{(\mathrm{tr}X)^2}.
```
Set $`B=X^{-1/2}VX^{-1/2}`$. Then the first trace is $`\mathrm{tr}(B^2)`$, and $`\mathrm{tr}V=\mathrm{tr}(XB)`$. Frobenius Cauchy–Schwarz and positive eigenvalues give
```math
(\mathrm{tr}(XB))^2\le\mathrm{tr}(X^2)\mathrm{tr}(B^2)
\le(\mathrm{tr}X)^2\mathrm{tr}(B^2).
```
Thus the directional second derivative is nonpositive on every line in the positive definite cone. The logarithm is concave, proving the matrix claim without assuming that $`X`$ and $`V`$ commute.
