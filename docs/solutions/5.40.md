# Exercise 5.40: A simple SDP dual for E-optimal design

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.40](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=300). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Let $`M(x)=\sum_i x_iv_iv_i^T`$. For $`M\succ0`$, $`\lambda_{\max}(M^{-1})=1/\lambda_{\min}(M)`$, so the lifted problem minimizes $`1/t`$ under $`M(x)\succeq tI`$, $`t>0`$, and the simplex constraints.

Use $`Z\succeq0`$ for $`tI-M(x)\preceq0`$, $`\nu`$ for $`\mathbf1^Tx=1`$, and keep $`x\succeq0`$ in the domain. The Lagrangian is

```math
1/t+t\mathrm{tr}Z+\sum_i x_i(\nu-v_i^TZv_i)-\nu.
```

Infimization over $`x`$ requires $`v_i^TZv_i\le\nu`$. For $`a=\mathrm{tr}Z\ge0`$, $`\inf_{t>0}(1/t+at)=2\sqrt a`$, attained at $`t=1/\sqrt a`$ for $`a>0`$ and approached as $`t\to\infty`$ for $`a=0`$. Thus a dual is

```math
\mathrm{maximize}\quad2\sqrt{\mathrm{tr}Z}-\nu\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad Z\succeq0,\quad v_i^TZv_i\le\nu.
```

If the vectors span, nonzero $`Z`$ forces $`\nu>0`$. Write $`Z=\nu W`$; the constraints are $`W\succeq0,v_i^TWv_i\le1`$. For fixed $`W`$, maximizing $`2\sqrt{\nu\mathrm{tr}W}-\nu`$ gives $`\nu=\mathrm{tr}W`$ and value $`\mathrm{tr}W`$. Hence the simplified dual is the SDP

```math
\mathrm{maximize}\quad\mathrm{tr}W\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad W\succeq0,\quad v_i^TWv_i\le1\quad\text{for all }i.
```

Recover the unsimplified pair by $`Z=(\mathrm{tr}W)W,\nu=\mathrm{tr}W`$. The zero matrix corresponds to the zero pair and is included by continuity.

For spanning vectors, uniform positive weights give $`M\succ0`$ and sufficiently small positive $`t`$ gives strict conic feasibility. Compactness of the design simplex and positivity of the best smallest eigenvalue yield a finite attained primal optimum; Slater yields an attained dual optimum and equality. Without spanning, the primal is infeasible and the normalized SDP is unbounded along $`W=svv^T`$ for a nonzero vector orthogonal to every design vector, consistently with value $`+\infty`$.

## Validation

The scalar infimum, scaling normalization, zero-trace case, and spanning versus nonspanning alternatives are checked.
