# Exercise 4.53: The convex upper image and its minimal elements

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 4.53](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=220). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Let $`X`$ be the convex feasible set and $`f_0`$ its $`K`$-convex vector objective. Take $`a=f_0(x)+k`$ and $`b=f_0(y)+l`$ in $`A=O+K`$, with $`k,l\in K`$. For $`0\le\theta\le1`$, the point $`z=\theta x+(1-\theta)y`$ is feasible and

```math
f_0(z)\preceq_K\theta f_0(x)+(1-\theta)f_0(y)
\preceq_K\theta a+(1-\theta)b.
```

The residual difference belongs to $`K`$ by its convex-cone properties. Hence the combination lies in $`A`$, proving convexity even if $`O`$ itself is not convex.

If $`a\in A`$ is minimal, write it as $`a=o+k`$ with $`o\in O,k\in K`$. Since $`o\in A`$ and $`o\preceq_K a`$, minimality forces $`a=o`$. Any distinct $`O`$-value dominating it would also be in $`A`$, so it is minimal in $`O`$.

Conversely, suppose $`o\in O`$ is minimal in $`O`$, and $`a\in A`$ satisfies $`a\preceq_K o`$. Write $`a=o'+k`$ with $`o'\in O,k\in K`$. Then $`o'\preceq_K a\preceq_K o`$, so original minimality gives $`o'=o`$. Now $`a-o=k\in K`$ and $`o-a\in K`$; pointedness implies $`k=0`$ and $`a=o`$. Thus $`o`$ is minimal in $`A`$.

## Validation

Convexity is used for the upper-image claim; pointedness is used to establish equality of the two minimal-element sets.
