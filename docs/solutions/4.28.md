# Exercise 4.28: Three robust quadratic-program formulations

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 4.28](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=212). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

## (a) Finite positive semidefinite scenarios

Introduce $`t`$ for the worst quadratic part and solve the convex QCQP

```math
\mathrm{minimize}\quad t+q^Tx+r\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad\tfrac12x^TP_kx\le t\ (k=1,\ldots,K),\quad Ax\preceq b.
```

For fixed $`x`$ the smallest $`t`$ is the maximum scenario value, proving exactness. Each constraint is convex because $`P_k\succeq0`$. Factoring the matrices and using rotated cones also gives an SOCP.

## (b) Spectrally bounded perturbation

Assume $`\gamma\ge0`$, as required for a nonempty stated uncertainty set. For every allowed perturbation $`\Delta`$,

```math
x^T\Delta x\le\gamma\|x\|_2^2,
```

and equality is attained by $`\Delta=\gamma I`$. The robust problem is therefore the QP with objective

```math
\tfrac12x^T(P_0+\gamma I)x+q^Tx+r
```

and the unchanged linear inequalities. It is convex because $`P_0+\gamma I\succeq0`$. If $`\gamma<0`$, the two matrix bounds are inconsistent; that is an empty uncertainty set, not the usual robust problem.

## (c) Ellipsoidal coefficient uncertainty

Let $`z_i(x)=x^TP_ix`$ for $`i=1,\ldots,K`$. The Euclidean support function gives the exact worst-case objective

```math
\tfrac12x^TP_0x+\tfrac12\left\|(z_1(x),\ldots,z_K(x))\right\|_2+q^Tx+r.
```

Each $`z_i`$ is nonnegative and convex. The Euclidean norm is convex and nondecreasing in each coordinate on the nonnegative orthant, so this is convex. Notice that an individual uncertain matrix need not be positive semidefinite; convexity follows from the displayed worst-case expression and the specific $`P_i\succeq0`$ assumption.

For an SOCP, choose real square-root factors $`P_i=R_i^TR_i`$, introduce $`s_0,\ldots,s_K,t`$, and use

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{minimize}\quad&\tfrac12s_0+\tfrac12t+q^Tx+r\\
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad&Ax\preceq b,\qquad\|(s_1,\ldots,s_K)\|_2\le t,\\
&\|(2R_ix,s_i-1)\|_2\le s_i+1,\quad i=0,\ldots,K.
\end{aligned}
```

The last cones impose $`s_i\ge z_i(x)\ge0`$, using $`z_0=x^TP_0x`$. Monotonicity of the norm gives a lower bound on every lifted objective, and choosing all $`s_i=z_i(x)`$, $`t=\|(z_i)_{i=1}^K\|_2`$ gives equality. This proves exact feasibility projection, values, and recovery in all three cases.

## Validation

The uncertainty support functions were maximized exactly, signs and the one-half factors were preserved, and the nonempty-set requirement in (b) is explicit.
