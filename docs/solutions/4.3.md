# Exercise 4.3: a box-QP certificate

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, printed p. 189
([official PDF, p. 203](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=203)).
This is a proof exercise with concrete data; the numerical solve here is an
optional illustration, not a replacement for the proof. The supplied matrix,
linear term, constant, and candidate are used without modification.

## Exact argument

For the candidate $`\bar x=(1,1/2,-1)`$, direct multiplication gives
$`P\bar x+q=(-1,0,2)`$. The leading principal minors of $`P`$ are
$`13,77,100`$, all positive, so Sylvester's criterion gives $`P\succ0`$.

For every point in the box,

```math
(P\bar x+q)^T(x-\bar x)=1-x_1+2(x_3+1)\geq0.
```

Expanding the objective around the candidate gives

```math
f(x)-f(\bar x)=(P\bar x+q)^T(x-\bar x)
 +\tfrac12(x-\bar x)^TP(x-\bar x).
```

The first term is nonnegative on the box, and the second is strictly positive
unless $`x=\bar x`$. Thus the candidate is the unique minimizer and
$`p^\star=-173/8=-21.625`$.

Equivalently, with upper-bound constraints $`x-1\leq0`$ and lower-bound
constraints $`-x-1\leq0`$, valid multipliers are
$`\lambda_U=(1,0,0)`$ and $`\lambda_L=(0,0,2)`$. They are nonnegative,
satisfy complementarity, and give
$`P\bar x+q+\lambda_U-\lambda_L=0`$.

## Numerical check

Run `convex-solutions run 4.3 --output artifacts/4.3`. The module reports the
exact certificate, eigenvalues, feasibility and KKT residuals, and a separate
CVXPY box-QP solve. A representative CLARABEL solve differs by about
$`2\times10^{-8}`$ in objective and $`1.4\times10^{-8}`$ in the point.
Small solver-dependent changes in those errors are expected.

Tests also check the expansion for seeded box points and recompute all leading
principal minors rather than trusting the reported certificate constants.
