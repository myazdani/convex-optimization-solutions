# Exercise 5.19: A compact LP representation for largest-coordinate sums

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.19](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=292). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

## (a) Selecting the largest coordinates

Sort $`x`$ in decreasing order and set $`t=x_{[r]}`$. For any feasible $`0\preceq y\preceq1`$, $`\mathbf1^Ty=r`$,

```math
x^Ty=rt+\sum_i y_i(x_i-t)
\le rt+\sum_i\max(x_i-t,0)
=\sum_{j=1}^r x_{[j]}.
```

The last identity includes ties at the threshold. Choosing $`y`$ equal to one on any $`r`$ largest indices and zero elsewhere attains equality. Thus the LP value is exactly the requested sum; tied coordinates allow fractional optimal selections.

## (b) Dual and epigraph

Keep $`y\succeq0`$ in the maximization domain, assign $`u\succeq0`$ to the upper bounds and a free $`t`$ to the budget equality. The upper-bounding Lagrangian is

```math
x^Ty+u^T(\mathbf1-y)+t(r-\mathbf1^Ty)
=\mathbf1^Tu+rt+y^T(x-u-t\mathbf1).
```

Its supremum over nonnegative $`y`$ is finite exactly when $`t\mathbf1+u\succeq x`$. Therefore the dual is the LP minimizing $`rt+\mathbf1^Tu`$ under that inequality and $`u\succeq0`$. The primal is feasible and compact, so LP strong duality and dual attainment apply, including $`r=n`$ when strict box feasibility is unavailable. An explicit optimal pair is the threshold above and $`u_i=\max(x_i-t,0)`$.

Consequently $`f(x)\le\alpha`$ is equivalent to existence of these auxiliary variables with $`rt+\mathbf1^Tu\le\alpha`$, exactly the stated $`2n+1`$ affine inequalities.

## (c) Diversified Markowitz QP

Let $`r=\lfloor0.1n\rfloor`$. Use variables $`x,u,t`$ and solve

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{minimize}\quad&x^T\Sigma x\\
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad&p^Tx\ge r_{\min},\quad\mathbf1^Tx=1,\quad x\succeq0,\\
&rt+\mathbf1^Tu\le0.8,\qquad t\mathbf1+u\succeq x,\quad u\succeq0.
\end{aligned}
```

The covariance $`\Sigma\succeq0`$ makes the objective convex quadratic, and all constraints are affine. The epigraph equivalence proves that no group of $`r`$ assets can hold more than the allowed budget fraction. If $`n<10`$, then $`r=0`$ and the literal empty-sum diversification constraint is vacuous; it may simply be omitted. The other constraints are unchanged.

## Validation

Threshold ties, the all-coordinate endpoint, LP dual attainment, and the floor-rounded zero-group case are explicit.
