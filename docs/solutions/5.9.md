# Exercise 5.9: A volume bound for a simple enclosing ellipsoid

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.9](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=289). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Put $`S=\sum_{k=1}^m a_ka_k^T`$. The spanning assumption gives $`S\succ0`$.

## (a) Feasibility

For each $`i`$, $`S-a_ia_i^T=\sum_{k\ne i}a_ka_k^T\succeq0`$. Hence

```math
\begin{bmatrix}S&a_i\\a_i^T&1\end{bmatrix}\succeq0
```

by the Schur complement of one. Taking instead the Schur complement of $`S\succ0`$ yields $`1-a_i^TS^{-1}a_i\ge0`$. Therefore $`X_{\mathrm{sim}}=S^{-1}\succ0`$ satisfies every containment inequality.

## (b) Optimizing a uniform dual weight

At $`\lambda=t\mathbf1`$, $`t>0`$, the dual objective is

```math
g(t\mathbf1)=\log\det S+n\log t-mt+n.
```

Its derivative $`n/t-m`$ vanishes uniquely at $`t=n/m`$, and its second derivative is negative. The best bound in this family is

```math
g((n/m)\mathbf1)=\log\det S+n\log(n/m).
```

The simple feasible point has primal objective $`f_0(X_{\mathrm{sim}})=\log\det S`$. Weak duality therefore gives

```math
0\le f_0(X_{\mathrm{sim}})-p^\star\le n\log(m/n).
```

An ellipsoid $`\{u:u^TXu\le1\}`$ has volume $`\kappa_n(\det X)^{-1/2}=\kappa_n e^{f_0(X)/2}`$. Consequently

```math
\frac{\mathrm{vol}(E_{\mathrm{sim}})}{\mathrm{vol}(E_{\mathrm{opt}})}
=\exp\left(\frac{f_0(X_{\mathrm{sim}})-p^\star}{2}\right)
\le(m/n)^{n/2}.
```

For completeness, an optimal ellipsoid exists: the spanning constraints bound $`X\succeq0`$ in norm, since $`\mathrm{tr}(XS)\le m`$ and $`S\succ0`$. The closed feasible PSD set is compact; maximizing determinant there has a positive value because a sufficiently small positive multiple of the identity is feasible. Its maximizer is positive definite. Thus the displayed optimal volume is attained. When $`m=n`$, the bound equals one, so the simple completion is optimal.

## Validation

The determinant-to-volume conversion includes the factor one half, and the spanning assumption supplies both invertibility and existence.
