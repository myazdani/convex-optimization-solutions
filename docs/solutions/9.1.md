# Exercise 9.1: Unbounded quadratic minimization

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 9.1, printed page 514. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=528).

## (a) Negative curvature
If symmetric $`P`$ is not positive semidefinite, choose $`v\ne0`$ with $`v^TPv<0`$. Along $`x=tv`$,
```math
f(tv)=\tfrac12t^2v^TPv+tq^Tv+r\longrightarrow-\infty
\quad(t\to+\infty),
```
since the negative quadratic term dominates the linear term.

## (b) A linear term outside the quadratic range
For symmetric $`P\succeq0`$, solvability of $`Px=-q`$ is equivalent to $`q\perp\ker P`$. If it fails, let $`v`$ be the nonzero orthogonal projection of $`q`$ onto $`\ker P`$. Then $`Pv=0`$ and $`q^Tv=\|v\|^2>0`$. Along $`x=-tv`$,
```math
f(-tv)=r-t\|v\|^2\longrightarrow-\infty.
```
For comparison, in the remaining case the minimizers are $`-P^\dagger q+\ker P`$, with finite value $`r-\tfrac12q^TP^\dagger q`$; this completes the possible quadratic cases.
