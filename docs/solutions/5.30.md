# Exercise 5.30: A log-determinant matrix secant update

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.30](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=297). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

The equality $`s^Ty=1`$ implies $`s\ne0`$. Use a multiplier $`\nu\in\mathbb R^n`$ for $`Xs-y=0`$. On symmetric matrices the derivative of $`\nu^TXs`$ is the symmetric matrix $`(\nu s^T+s\nu^T)/2`$. Thus the KKT conditions are

```math
X\succ0,\qquad Xs=y,\qquad
I-X^{-1}+\tfrac12(\nu s^T+s\nu^T)=0.
```

There are no inequality multipliers for the positive-definite domain.

Let $`S=s^Ts`$ and consider $`X^\star=I+yy^T-ss^T/S`$. Its secant equality follows immediately:

```math
X^\star s=s+y(y^Ts)-s=y.
```

For every nonzero vector $`v`$,

```math
v^TX^\star v=\|v\|_2^2-\frac{(s^Tv)^2}{S}+(y^Tv)^2>0.
```

Both terms after orthogonal decomposition are nonnegative. Equality would require $`v`$ parallel to $`s`$ and orthogonal to $`y`$, which is impossible for nonzero $`v`$ because $`s^Ty=1`$. Thus $`X^\star\succ0`$.

Direct multiplication using $`s^Ty=1`$ verifies

```math
(X^\star)^{-1}=I-sy^T-ys^T+(1+y^Ty)ss^T.
```

Choose $`\nu^\star=-2y+(1+y^Ty)s`$. Its symmetric outer product with $`s`$ equals $`(X^\star)^{-1}-I`$, so stationarity holds. The KKT conditions are therefore satisfied. The objective is strictly convex on the positive-definite cone because $`-\log\det X`$ has positive second variation in every nonzero symmetric direction. Convexity makes this KKT certificate sufficient, and strict convexity makes the feasible optimum unique.

## Validation

The symmetric derivative includes its one-half factor, positive definiteness is proved rather than inferred from feasibility, and the inverse identity gives an explicit dual certificate.
