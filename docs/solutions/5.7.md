# Exercise 5.7: Piecewise-linear duals and log-sum-exp approximation

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.7](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=288). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Let $`A`$ have rows $`a_i^T`$ and let $`b=(b_i)`$.

## (a) Equality-lift dual

Use $`L(x,y,\lambda)=\max_i y_i+\lambda^T(Ax+b-y)`$. Infimizing in $`x`$ requires $`A^T\lambda=0`$. The remaining infimum $`\inf_y(\max_i y_i-\lambda^Ty)`$ equals zero if $`\lambda\succeq0,\mathbf1^T\lambda=1`$: the weighted average never exceeds the maximum, and constant $`y`$ attains equality. If the sum of weights is not one, shift every coordinate equally to make it unbounded below. If some weight is negative, send that coordinate to $`-\infty`$ with the others zero. Thus the dual is

```math
\mathrm{maximize}\quad b^T\lambda\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad A^T\lambda=0,\quad
\lambda\succeq0,\quad\mathbf1^T\lambda=1.
```

## (b) LP epigraph dual

The primal LP minimizes $`t`$ subject to $`Ax+b\preceq t\mathbf1`$. Its Lagrangian is $`t(1-\mathbf1^T\lambda)+x^TA^T\lambda+b^T\lambda`$ with $`\lambda\succeq0`$. Infima over unrestricted $`x,t`$ impose exactly the two equalities above, giving the same dual. The epigraph LP is always feasible and strictly feasible; when its value is finite, LP strong duality applies. A finite optimum is attained.

## (c) Unit-temperature smooth approximation

For any real vector $`z`$, put $`M=\max_i z_i`$. Since $`1\le\sum_i e^{z_i-M}\le m`$,

```math
M\le\log\sum_i e^{z_i}\le M+\log m.
```

Apply this with $`z=Ax+b`$ and take infima. For finite optimal values,

```math
0\le p^\star_{\mathrm{gp}}-p^\star_{\mathrm{pwl}}\le\log m.
```

The smooth dual makes the same bound transparent: it has the same feasible simplex slice as in (a), but objective $`b^T\lambda-\sum_i\lambda_i\log\lambda_i`$. The entropy term lies in $`[0,\log m]`$, with $`0\log0=0`$.

## (d) General temperature

Replace $`z`$ by $`\gamma z`$ and divide by $`\gamma>0`$ to get a uniform approximation error at most $`(\log m)/\gamma`$. Therefore

```math
0\le p^\star_{\gamma}-p^\star_{\mathrm{pwl}}
\le\frac{\log m}{\gamma}.
```

The dual entropy coefficient is likewise $`1/\gamma`$. As $`\gamma\to\infty`$, optimal values converge to the piecewise-linear optimum and every attained smoothed optimizer is at most $`(\log m)/\gamma`$ suboptimal for the original problem. This does not guarantee convergence of optimizer vectors without compactness or uniqueness. If the piecewise-linear infimum is $`-\infty`$, all smoothed infima are also $`-\infty`$ by the pointwise bound; their difference is undefined, so the finite-gap notation is interpreted only for finite values.
