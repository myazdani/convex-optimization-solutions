# Exercise 7.2: Uniform noise with unknown width

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 7.2, printed page 393. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=407).

For $`m\ge1`$ independent measurements and $`\alpha>0`$, the likelihood is
```math
L(x,\alpha)=(2\alpha)^{-m}\,
\mathbf1_{\{\|Ax-y\|_\infty\le\alpha\}}.
```
On its positive domain it decreases strictly with $`\alpha`$. Thus, when $`r_*:=\min_x\|Ax-y\|_\infty>0`$, the ML estimates are precisely the pairs $`(x_*,r_*)`$ with $`x_*`$ solving the infinity-norm approximation problem. That problem is the LP minimizing $`t`$ subject to $`-t\mathbf1\le Ax-y\le t\mathbf1`$.

The minimum residual exists since an affine subspace is closed and distance to it in finite dimension is attained. If $`r_*=0`$, the likelihood is unbounded as $`\alpha\downarrow0`$ at an exact fit. There is then no finite-width ML estimate in the stated density family: saying $`\hat\alpha=0`$ means a boundary/degenerate limit, not an admissible uniform density. This is the only qualification needed for the stated equivalence.
