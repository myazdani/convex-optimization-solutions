# Exercise 3.54: A direct proof for the Gaussian cumulative distribution

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.54, p. 123](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=137).

Original solution.

Write the normal density as $`\varphi(x)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-x^2/2}`$ and its CDF as $`F`$. Then $`F'=\varphi`$ and $`F''=-x\varphi`$.

## (a) Nonnegative arguments

For $`x\ge0`$, $`F''(x)\le0`$ and $`F(x)>0`$, so $`F''(x)F(x)\le0\le(F'(x))^2`$.

## (b) Quadratic tangent bound

The inequality $`(t-x)^2\ge0`$ rearranges to $`t^2/2\ge xt-x^2/2`$ for every real $`t,x`$.

## (c) Tail integral bound

Negating and exponentiating the preceding inequality gives $`e^{-t^2/2}\le e^{x^2/2-xt}`$. Integrate from minus infinity to $`x`$. If $`x<0`$, the exponential integral converges and equals
```math
\int_{-\infty}^x e^{-t^2/2}\,dt
\le e^{x^2/2}\int_{-\infty}^x e^{-xt}\,dt
=\frac{e^{-x^2/2}}{-x}.
```
Hence $`F(x)\le\varphi(x)/(-x)`$ for negative $`x`$.

## (d) Negative arguments and conclusion

For $`x<0`$, the factor $`-x\varphi(x)`$ is positive, so multiplying the bound gives
```math
F''(x)F(x)=(-x)\varphi(x)F(x)\le\varphi(x)^2=(F'(x))^2.
```
The point zero was already covered in (a). Since $`F>0`$ everywhere, $`(\log F)''=(F''F-(F')^2)/F^2\le0`$. This proves the claimed log-concavity on the entire line.
