# Exercise 8.17: Invariance properties of the analytic center

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 8.17, printed page 449. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=463).

For strict linear inequalities $`a_i^Tx<b_i`$, the analytic center minimizes $`\phi(x)=-\sum_i\log(b_i-a_i^Tx)`$. Under an invertible affine coordinate change $`x=Tz+c`$, the transformed inequalities have slacks
```math
(b_i-a_i^Tc)-(T^Ta_i)^Tz=b_i-a_i^T(Tz+c).
```
Their barrier is exactly $`\phi(Tz+c)`$. Because the coordinate map is a bijection, its minimizers are exactly $`z=T^{-1}(x-c)`$ for analytic centers $`x`$ of the original system. This proves affine invariance, including nonunique minimizers if the description has lineality.

Multiplying inequality $`i`$ by $`\alpha_i>0`$ changes its barrier term to
```math
-\log(\alpha_i(b_i-a_i^Tx))
=-\log(b_i-a_i^Tx)-\log\alpha_i.
```
The total barrier changes by a constant, leaving the analytic center unchanged. The same argument applies to general inequality barriers when their slack functions are composed with the coordinate change and positively rescaled. No claim is made for singular coordinate maps, negative scaling, or replacing the description by additional redundant inequalities.
