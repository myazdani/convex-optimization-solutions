# Exercise 8.6: Convexity of distances between translated and scaled sets

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 8.6, printed page 448. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=462).

Assume both sets are nonempty; if one is empty the distance is identically $`+\infty`$.

## (a) Translation
For $`x_1,x_2`$ choose $`c_i\in C,d_i\in D`$ with $`\|c_i-x_i-d_i\|`$ within $`\epsilon`$ of the corresponding infimum. Convexity of both sets and the triangle inequality give
```math
\mathrm{dist}(C,\theta x_1+(1-\theta)x_2+D)
\le\theta\|c_1-x_1-d_1\|+(1-\theta)\|c_2-x_2-d_2\|.
```
Let $`\epsilon\downarrow0`$ to obtain the convexity inequality. Attainment and closedness are unnecessary.

## (b) Translation and common scale
Positive homogeneity of the norm gives
```math
\mathrm{dist}(tC,x+tD)=t\,\mathrm{dist}(C,x/t+D),\qquad t>0.
```
To check convexity directly, set $`t=\theta t_1+(1-\theta)t_2`$ and $`\alpha=\theta t_1/t`$. Then
```math
\frac{\theta x_1+(1-\theta)x_2}{t}
=\alpha\frac{x_1}{t_1}+(1-\alpha)\frac{x_2}{t_2}.
```
Apply part (a) with weights $`\alpha,1-\alpha`$ and multiply by $`t`$. This gives precisely the joint convexity inequality in $`(x,t)`$.
