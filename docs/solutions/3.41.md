# Exercise 3.41: Conjugate of negative normalized entropy

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.41, p. 121](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=135).

Original solution.

Write every positive vector as $`x=sp`$, where $`s=\mathbf1^Tx>0`$ and $`p>0,\mathbf1^Tp=1`$. Then
```math
y^Tx-f(x)=s\left(y^Tp-\sum_i p_i\log p_i\right).
```
Let $`Z=\sum_i e^{y_i}`$ and $`q_i=e^{y_i}/Z>0`$. The bracket is
```math
y^Tp-\sum_i p_i\log p_i
=\log Z-\sum_i p_i\log(p_i/q_i)\le\log Z,
```
by nonnegativity of the probability KL divergence, with equality at $`p=q`$. Therefore if $`Z\le1`$, all maximands are nonpositive and their supremum is zero by $`s\downarrow0`$. If $`Z>1`$, choose $`p=q`$ and let $`s\to\infty`$ to obtain infinity. Hence
```math
f^*(y)=\begin{cases}0,&\sum_i e^{y_i}\le1,\\+\infty,&\sum_i e^{y_i}>1.
\end{cases}
```
At $`Z=1`$ every vector on the ray $`x=sq`$ attains zero; at $`Z<1`$ the finite supremum is approached at the excluded zero vector and is not attained.
