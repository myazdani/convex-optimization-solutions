# Exercise 5.15: Dual certificates from increasing convex penalties

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.15](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=291). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Because each $`h_i`$ is convex and nondecreasing and each $`f_i`$ is convex, the composition is convex. Explicitly,

```math
h_i(f_i(\theta x+(1-\theta)y))
\le h_i(\theta f_i(x)+(1-\theta)f_i(y))
\le\theta h_i(f_i(x))+(1-\theta)h_i(f_i(y)).
```

Adding these inequalities and the convex objective proves convexity of $`\phi`$.

At its unconstrained minimizer $`\widetilde x`$, differentiability gives

```math
0=\nabla f_0(\widetilde x)+
\sum_i h_i'(f_i(\widetilde x))\nabla f_i(\widetilde x).
```

Set $`\widetilde\lambda_i=h_i'(f_i(\widetilde x))\ge0`$. The nonnegative sign follows from monotonicity. The original Lagrangian $`L(x,\widetilde\lambda)=f_0(x)+\sum_i\widetilde\lambda_i f_i(x)`$ is convex in $`x`$, and its gradient vanishes at $`\widetilde x`$, so its infimum is attained there. Consequently

```math
p^\star\ge g(\widetilde\lambda)
=f_0(\widetilde x)+\sum_i h_i'(f_i(\widetilde x))f_i(\widetilde x).
```

This is an evaluated dual-feasible lower bound, irrespective of whether the penalty minimizer is primal feasible. A zero derivative is permitted. No Slater or strong-duality assumption is required for this certificate; convexity and the attained differentiable penalty minimizer suffice.

## Validation

The penalty values themselves are not the Lagrange coefficients: their derivatives at the constraint residuals are. The proof establishes global Lagrangian minimization, not just dual sign feasibility.
