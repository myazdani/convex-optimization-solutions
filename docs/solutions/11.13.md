# Exercise 11.13: Entropy becomes self-concordant after adding a log barrier

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 11.13, printed page 627. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=641).

## (a) Negative entropy alone
For $`f(x)=x\log x`$, $`f''=1/x`$ and $`|f'''|=1/x^2`$. The self-concordance ratio is
```math
\frac{|f'''|}{2(f'')^{3/2}}=\frac1{2\sqrt x},
```
which exceeds one for $`0<x<1/4`$ and is unbounded near zero. Thus the function is not self-concordant on the positive halfline.

## (b) Barrier-augmented entropy
For $`F(x)=t x\log x-\log x`$, $`t>0`$,
```math
F''(x)=\frac{tx+1}{x^2},\qquad
|F'''(x)|=\frac{tx+2}{x^3}.
```
With $`z=tx\ge0`$, the required inequality is $`z+2\le2(z+1)^{3/2}`$. Both sides are nonnegative, and squaring gives
```math
4(z+1)^3-(z+2)^2=4z^3+11z^2+8z\ge0.
```
Therefore $`|F'''|\le2(F'')^{3/2}`$ for every positive $`x`$, proving self-concordance for every $`t>0`$.
