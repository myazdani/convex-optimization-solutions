# Exercise 7.10: A covariance lower bound as an LMI

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 7.10, printed page 395. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=409).

Let $`A=[\alpha_1\ \cdots\ \alpha_n]`$, $`\mu=Ap`$, and $`M=\sum_ip_i\alpha_i\alpha_i^T`$. The covariance is $`M-\mu\mu^T`$. Its lower bound by a given symmetric $`S`$ is equivalent, by completing the square or the Schur complement of the scalar one, to
```math
\begin{bmatrix}
\sum_ip_i\alpha_i\alpha_i^T-S&Ap\\
p^TA^T&1
\end{bmatrix}\succeq0.
```
Every entry is affine in $`p`$. Together with $`p\ge0,\ \mathbf1^Tp=1`$, this is a convex constraint on the probability vector. No independence assumptions or positive definiteness of $`S`$ are required for this equivalence.
