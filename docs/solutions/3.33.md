# Exercise 3.33: Direct proof of perspective convexity

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.33, p. 119](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=133).

Original solution.

Let $`g(x,t)=t f(x/t)`$ with $`t>0`$ and $`x/t\in\mathrm{dom}\,f`$. Take two domain points $`(x,t),(y,s)`$ and a weight $`0<\theta<1`$. Set
```math
r=\theta t+(1-\theta)s>0,\qquad
\alpha=\frac{\theta t}{r},\qquad
\beta=\frac{(1-\theta)s}{r}.
```
The weights are nonnegative and sum to one, and
```math
\frac{\theta x+(1-\theta)y}{r}
=\alpha\frac xt+\beta\frac ys.
```
Convexity of the original domain proves that the combined point belongs to the perspective domain. Applying convexity of $`f`$ to the last display and multiplying by $`r>0`$ gives
```math
g(\theta x+(1-\theta)y,r)
\le r\alpha f(x/t)+r\beta f(y/s)
=\theta g(x,t)+(1-\theta)g(y,s).
```
Weights zero and one give equality and require no special denominator manipulation. This proves both domain convexity and the defining convexity inequality.
