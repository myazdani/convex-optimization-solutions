# Exercise 3.23: Two perspective constructions

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization, exercise 3.23, p. 117](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=131).

Original solution.

## (a) Power divided by a power of scale

For $`p>1`$, $`h(z)=\sum_i|z_i|^p`$ is convex. Its perspective is
```math
t h(x/t)=t\sum_i|x_i/t|^p
=\frac{\|x\|_p^p}{t^{p-1}},\qquad t>0.
```
The perspective theorem therefore proves convexity on the stated domain. The argument works for $`1<p<2`$ even where a second derivative of the scalar power is unavailable.

## (b) Affine quadratic-over-linear function

The perspective of $`h(z)=\|z\|_2^2`$ is $`g(z,t)=\|z\|_2^2/t`$, convex for $`t>0`$. Compose it with the affine map $`x\mapsto(Ax+b,c^Tx+d)`$. Its inverse-image domain is the open halfspace $`c^Tx+d>0`$, which is convex, and the resulting function is exactly the one requested. Neither matrix rank nor a nonzero $`c`$ is required; the formula covers full or empty domains when $`c=0`$.
