# Exercise 5.17: Exact LP robustification of polyhedral row uncertainty

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 5.17](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=292). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Fix a row and a proposed decision vector $`x`$. Its support problem is

```math
\mathrm{maximize}_a\quad x^Ta\qquad\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad C_i a\preceq d_i.
```

Its LP dual is

```math
\mathrm{minimize}_{z_i}\quad d_i^Tz_i\qquad
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad C_i^Tz_i=x,\quad z_i\succeq0.
```

If the support is bounded above, nonemptiness of the row polyhedron and LP strong duality give an attained dual optimum equal to that support. Thus the robust inequality $`\sup_{a\in P_i}a^Tx\le b_i`$ holds if and only if there exists $`z_i\succeq0`$ with $`C_i^Tz_i=x`$ and $`d_i^Tz_i\le b_i`$.

The reverse implication can also be seen directly: $`a^Tx=z_i^TC_i a\le z_i^Td_i\le b_i`$ for every allowed row. If the support is $`+\infty`$, no such multiplier exists, since that direct inequality would bound it. Therefore boundedness of the uncertainty polyhedron itself is unnecessary.

Apply this equivalence to every row independently and keep the same objective $`c^Tx`$. The resulting LP is exactly the one in the exercise. Feasible points project precisely to robust-feasible decisions; every robust-feasible decision has attained dual certificates for every row. The objective is unchanged, so values and optimizer recovery agree, including infeasibility or unbounded outer minimization.

## Validation

The nonempty uncertainty-set assumption is used for necessity; unbounded uncertainty sets and infinite supports are treated rather than assuming compactness.
