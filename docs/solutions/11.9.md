# Exercise 11.9: A dual certificate near a quadratic central path

Source: Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, exercise 11.9, printed page 625. [Official book](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=639).

Write $`s_i=-f_i(x)>0`$, $`a_i=\nabla f_i(x)=P_ix+q_i`$, and let $`v=\Delta x_{\rm nt}`$. The proposed multipliers are
```math
\lambda_i=\frac1{t s_i}\left(1+\frac{a_i^Tv}{s_i}\right).
```
They are positive whenever $`|a_i^Tv|<s_i`$ for every $`i`$, an explicit sufficient small-step condition. Define $`x_0=x+v`$ and $`x_i=x+v/(t\lambda_i s_i)`$. Quadratic gradients are affine, so
```math
\nabla f_0(x_0)=\nabla f_0(x)+P_0v,
\qquad \lambda_i\nabla f_i(x_i)=\lambda_i a_i+\frac{P_iv}{t s_i}.
```
Expanding $`\lambda_i a_i`$ and summing gives
```math
\nabla f_0(x_0)+\sum_i\lambda_i\nabla f_i(x_i)
=\nabla f_0(x)+\sum_i\frac{a_i}{t s_i}
+\left(P_0+\sum_i\frac{P_i}{t s_i}
+\sum_i\frac{a_i a_i^T}{t s_i^2}\right)v=0.
```
The final equality is exactly the centering Newton equation divided by $`t`$.

For any $`z`$, apply the convex first-order lower bound to each quadratic at its corresponding $`x_i`$ and multiply by its nonnegative multiplier. The coefficients of $`z`$ cancel by the identity above, giving the finite global lower bound
```math
L(z,\lambda)\ge
f_0(x_0)-\nabla f_0(x_0)^Tx_0
+\sum_i\lambda_i[f_i(x_i)-\nabla f_i(x_i)^Tx_i].
```
Thus the Lagrangian is bounded below, and $`\lambda\ge0`$ is dual feasible in the finite-dual-value sense used here. The points $`x_i`$ need not be primal feasible; global supporting inequalities for convex quadratics hold everywhere. The argument deliberately does not claim the original $`x`$ minimizes the Lagrangian or that its gap is still $`m/t`$.
