# Exercise 4.27: A matrix-fractional objective as an SOCP

Source: [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, exercise 4.27](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=212). The source exercise and all of its subparts were checked directly.

Fix $`x\succeq0`$, let $`D=\mathrm{diag}(x)`$ and $`a=Ax+b`$, and write $`S=I+BDB^T\succ0`$. Let $`y=S^{-1}a`$. Define

```math
v=y,\qquad w=DB^Ty.
```

Then $`v+Bw=Sy=a`$, and $`w_i=0`$ whenever $`x_i=0`$. Their objective is

```math
\|v\|_2^2+\sum_{i:x_i>0}\frac{w_i^2}{x_i}
=y^T(I+BDB^T)y=a^TS^{-1}a.
```

For any other finite-cost feasible $`v,w`$, completing squares gives

```math
\|v\|_2^2+\sum_{i:x_i>0}\frac{w_i^2}{x_i}-a^TS^{-1}a
=\|v-y\|_2^2+
\sum_{i:x_i>0}\frac{(w_i-x_i b_i^Ty)^2}{x_i}\ge0,
```

where $`b_i`$ is column $`i`$ of $`B`$. The cross terms cancel because $`v+Bw=a`$. This proves the hint's variational identity, including zero coordinates.

Introduce nonnegative epigraph variables $`t_0,t_1,\ldots,t_n`$. An exact SOCP is

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{minimize}\quad&t_0+\sum_i t_i\\
\mathrm{subject\ to}\quad&v+Bw=Ax+b,\qquad x\succeq0,\\
&\|(2v,t_0-1)\|_2\le t_0+1,\\
&\|(2w_i,t_i-x_i)\|_2\le t_i+x_i,\quad i=1,\ldots,n.
\end{aligned}
```

The first cone represents $`\|v\|_2^2\le t_0`$. Each subsequent cone represents $`w_i^2\le t_ix_i`$ with $`t_i,x_i\ge0`$. At $`x_i=0`$ it forces $`w_i=0`$, and the minimum epigraph cost is zero, exactly the prescribed closed quadratic-over-linear convention.

Every original point has the equality-attaining lift above. Every conic feasible point has objective at least the original matrix-fractional value at its $`x`$. Therefore values and optimizers correspond in both directions. The proof does not assume the original problem attains its infimum on the unbounded nonnegative orthant.

## Validation

The identity is proved by a square completion, not by inverting a possibly singular $`D`$. The matrix $`I+BDB^T`$ remains positive definite for every feasible $`x`$.
